Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

1 About Freezit

Freezit is a household food inventory app that helps you track frozen items and manage expiry dates. This policy describes the personal data we collect and how it is handled.

2 Data We Collect

We collect the following information: • Your email address, to create and manage your login account. • Food inventory data you enter: item names, categories, quantities, and expiry dates. • Household data: your household name, join code, and the names and email addresses of members you invite. • Activity history: a record of who added, removed, or moved items within your household.

3 How We Use Your Data

Your data is used exclusively to operate the Freezit service — to display your inventory, share it with your household members, and surface expiry alerts. We do not use your data for advertising, and we do not sell or share it with third parties for commercial purposes.

4 Data Sharing

Food inventory and activity data is visible to all members of your household. By joining or creating a household, you consent to this sharing. No data is shared outside your household except as required by law.

5 Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored using Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore for data, Firebase Authentication for accounts). Firebase provides industry-standard encryption and security. Data may be stored on servers located outside your country of residence.

6 Deleting Your Data

You can delete your account at any time from Settings → Delete Account. If you are the last member of your household, all data — including freezers, drawers, items, and activity history — will be permanently and immediately erased. If other members remain, only your personal account is removed and household data is preserved.

7 Contact

For questions about this policy or to request a copy of your data, contact us at: evan@wilsonasc.co.nz
